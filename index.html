II. The Case Room · Case VII

The Drowned Hour

A novel, The Drowned Hour (1894), by Cornelia Ashgrove. Its heroine, Isolde Marne, is lost at the boathouse of Marrow End on the night of the fourteenth of October. The inquest chapter says it was an accident. The Society is not certain the page has told us everything.

Open each item. Your notebook keeps what you find. Select two entries in it to test whether they bear on each other.
